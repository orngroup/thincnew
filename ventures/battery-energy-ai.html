The approach

Store at the right time.
Use when it matters.

Electricity prices and building demand change throughout the day. Battery storage can shift consumption away from higher-priced periods and help manage peak demand.

Working with technology and operating partners, we bring site origination, capital and commercial structure to suitable projects.

01

02

Size storage and control systems around the site’s technical and commercial requirements.

03

Use consumption and pricing information to inform charging and discharge decisions.
